## About me

I am an undergraduate student in **Computer Science and Robotics** at **Heriot-Watt University, Edinburgh** and **Ocean University of China**. I am interested in building reliable, efficient, and collaborative intelligent systems.

- 🔬 Researching **federated learning**, **distributed learning**, **computer networks**, and **computer vision**.
